Infrastructure

The spaces where our students learn and play.

School building

A bright three-level building with separate blocks for different groups of students, furnished with modern conveniences.

A technology-enabled classroom with a projector and smart board, where ICT supports learning and classroom discussion.

Computer lab

A lab with current hardware and software and skilled staff to guide students through IT-enhanced learning.

Library

A place where teachers and students explore subjects and topics in depth, with sections for English, Hindi, Assamese and general knowledge.

Our newly built library, with bright reading tables and tall shelves of reference books, encyclopedias and novels.
